# Emmanuil — User Guide

**Emmanuil** is your personal "relationship portfolio": one place to keep the people who matter and everything you know about them, with a smart assistant that reminds you when to reach out and what to ask. Instead of holding it all in your head, you record what you know about a person once — and the app reminds and prompts you at the right time.

The app runs in the browser and on your phone (you can install it to your home screen). The interface is available in English or Ukrainian.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open **emmanuil-project.vercel.app** in your browser.
2. Sign in one of two ways:
   - **Continue with Google** — sign in with your Google account (fastest).
   - **Email** — enter your email, get a sign-in link by mail, tap it.
3. That's it. Every user has their own private space: only you can see your data.

**Language.** At the bottom, open the **Settings** tab → **Language** → switch English ⇄ Ukrainian.

---

## 2. Install on your phone (recommended)

So you get reminders as normal phone notifications:

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open the site in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an up arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open the app **from the icon** on your home screen.
4. At the bottom → **Settings** → **Turn on notifications** → allow.

**Android (Chrome):** menu (⋮) → **Install app** → then Settings → **Turn on notifications**.

After that, one notification arrives each morning with who to message and who to wish.

---

## 3. The main sections

Four tabs at the bottom: **Today**, **People**, **Assistant**, **Settings**.

### 📅 Today

Your home screen — "people to reconnect with." The app gathers for today:

- **People it's time to reach out to** — based on your chosen rhythm; each already comes with a personal "what to ask" prompt (generated from the facts you saved).
- **Birthdays & key dates** — the person's own, and those you added (e.g. their son's birthday).
- **Follow-ups** — one-off tasks tied to a specific person.

Each item has **Done** (mark that you reached out) and **Snooze** (3 or 7 days).

If there's nothing for today, you'll see a calm "no one to reach out to — enjoy the calm."

### 👥 People

The list of all your contacts. At the top: **search by name** and **filter by relationship type**.

**Add a person:** the **Add person** button. Fields:
- **Full name** (required).
- **How we met**, **Location**, **Birthday**.
- **Tags** (comma-separated — e.g. "work, golf, investors").
- **Relationship type:** VIP / Friend / Acquaintance.
- **Photo.**

### 🤖 Assistant

A smart helper you talk to by **text or voice**. Two main things:

**1. Ask** about anyone:
> "What do I know about Maria?"

The assistant gives a short brief from what you've saved.

**2. Capture** something new in plain language:
> "Maria's son Andrii just started at LSE"

The assistant understands it, shows a **proposed update**, and asks you to confirm. **Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm** — so there are no accidental entries.

**Voice:** tap the **microphone**, dictate, and the app turns speech into text; you review it and send.

### ⚙️ Settings

Language, turning notifications on, signing out, and links to the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**.

## 4. The Person card — the heart of the app

Tap a person in the list to open their card. Everything lives here:

### "What do I know?" — AI brief
The button at the top of the card. In a moment the app builds a concise brief: a summary, talking points, what to ask about, and why it's worth reconnecting right now. Handy before a call or meeting.

### Facts
What you know about the person, grouped by category:
- **Family** (kids, spouse, "ask about their mother"…)
- **Work**
- **Projects**
- **Interests**
- **Ask about** (things worth raising in conversation)

Add facts with the button or via the assistant.

### Interactions
A timeline of your contact. Log a **call / meeting / message** with a short summary and date — so the history of the relationship is visible.

### Key dates
Birthdays and anniversaries of this person's family (e.g. "son's birthday — March 3"). They recur every year and appear in **Today** and in notifications with a ready-made greeting.

### Stay in touch (rhythm)
Here you set **how often** to be reminded about this person — e.g. every 14 / 30 / 90 days. When it's time, the person shows up in **Today** with a personal prompt. The **Done** button moves the next reminder forward.

### Reminders
A one-off task tied to a person: "remind me to send the article on the 20th."

## 5. How it works in practice — an example

1. You meet someone → add them under **People** (name, how you met, relationship type).
2. On their card you add a few **facts** (family, work) — by hand or by telling the assistant.
3. You set a **rhythm** of "every 30 days" and add a **key date** (their daughter's birthday).
4. A month later, a **notification** arrives in the morning: "Reach out to …" plus a prompt of what to ask.
5. You call → tap **Done** → log a short **interaction**.
6. Before the next contact you tap **"What do I know?"** — and recall every detail in a second.

## 6. Notifications (reminders)

- They arrive **once a day in the morning** as a single notification with who to message and who to wish.
- They work after you've **installed the app on your phone and turned notifications on** (section 2).
- Every user gets **their own** reminders, for their own people and rhythm.

## 7. Privacy and security

- Data is **not sold** and not used for advertising.
- AI features process text/voice through partner services only to produce a response; voice is not stored after transcription.
- The full text is in the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** (linked at the bottom of the sign-in screen and in Settings).

> You are responsible for the information you store about other people. Keep what's appropriate and respect their privacy.
